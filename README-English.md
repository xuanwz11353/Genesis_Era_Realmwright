# Genesis Era: Realmwright

A personal Minecraft 1.21.1 modpack, built on **NeoForge** (Fabric deprecated).

---

## Pack Overview

**Genesis Era: Realmwright** is an **epic NeoForge modpack** designed for players seeking the ultimate gaming experience. With "creation and exploration" as its core philosophy, this pack perfectly integrates magical mysteries, technological development, architectural artistry, and performance optimization to create a block world of infinite possibilities.

> *"Here, you are not just a passerby in the world, but the architect of all things."*

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 🔮 Magic & Mystery
- **Diverse Magic Systems**: Integrated Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, Goety, and more for a complete magical ecosystem
- **Dimensional Exploration**: The Twilight Forest, Deeper and Darker, The Bumblezone, and other mysterious dimensions await discovery
- **Enchantment Revolution**: Universal Enchants, Enchantment Level Break redefine equipment enhancement

### 🏗️ Architecture & Creation
- **Complete Building Suite**: Full Macaw series building mods, from doors and windows to furniture
- **Modern & Classical Fusion**: MrCrayfish's Refurbished Furniture complements Quark's decorative modules
- **Mechanical Power**: Complete Create mod with addons (Central Kitchen, Stuff 'N Additions, New Age, etc.) bringing automation and mechanical building experiences

### 🌍 World & Exploration
- **Biome Expansion**: Biomes O' Plenty, Oh The Biomes We've Gone, Regions Unexplored redefine world landscapes
- **Dungeon Enhancement**: Repurposed Structures comprehensively optimizes all structures; Moog's series adds new adventures
- **Mapping System**: Xaero series maps with Explorer's Compass help you navigate the world

### ⚡ Performance & Optimization
- **Triple Optimization Core**: Sodium + Lithium + FerriteCore ensure smooth experience
- **Smart Rendering**: Entity Culling boosts frame rates
- **Memory Management**: FerriteCore, AllTheLeaks optimize resource usage

### 🌾 Life & Agriculture
- **Culinary Paradise**: Farmer's Delight as core, with dozens of food expansion mods (Pam's HarvestCraft 2, Cultural Delights, Storage Delight, etc.)
- **Agricultural Revolution**: Croptopia, Naturalist enrich farming and ecological experiences
- **Culinary Arts**: From Rustic Delight to Pineapple Delight, creating unique food culture

## 📦 Installation

1. Use PCL / HMCL, drag the modpack file downloaded from GitHub into the launcher
   - Don't have a launcher? Recommended: [PCL2](https://github.com/Hex-Dragon/PCL2)
2. Refer to online tutorials for more details

---

## 💡 Play Suggestions

1. **Memory Allocation**: Allocate at least **8 GiB** of RAM, use **Java 21**
2. **Mod Modifications**: You may modify/upgrade mods as you like, but ensure mods support **1.21.1 NeoForge**. If you encounter issues after adding mods, please resolve them yourself
3. **Shaders**: Some shaders are pre-packaged; you can also add your own to the `shaderpacks` folder
4. **Issue Reporting**: If you encounter problems while playing, submit to Issues with the full game log (provided you haven't modified the modpack)
5. **Custom Music**: Find `GER自定义音乐` in the `resourcepacks` folder; refer to [this guide](https://github.com/CircuitLord/ReactiveMusic/blob/master/docs/MAKING_SONGPACKS.md) to customize your music
6. **JVM Arguments**: Add G1GC arguments to reduce stuttering (configurable via PCL)
